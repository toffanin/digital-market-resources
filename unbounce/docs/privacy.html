Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how the ARCHES Innovation Display Network ("we," "our," or "us") handles data and privacy across our promotional bridge pages under the ARCHES 4-D Innovation Framework.

1. Data Collection & Processing

We prioritize minimal data collection. This promotional bridge page does not directly request or store personal identifying information (PII) such as passwords or financial data on our servers.

  • Technical Logs: Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, referral URL, time stamp) may be collected automatically to maintain operational security and performance.
  • Affiliate Tracking: Clicking external links (such as Unbounce partner links) may place a standard HTTP tracking cookie on your device via third-party affiliate networks (e.g., PartnerStack) to attribute referrals.

2. GDPR Compliance & European Data Rights

In alignment with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards, EU residents hold explicit rights regarding personal data:

  • The right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data processed through our infrastructure.
  • The right to restrict or object to data processing.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a national Data Protection Authority (DPA).

3. Third-Party Links & External Platforms

This bridge page contains links to third-party software providers, specifically Unbounce and partner networks. Once you navigate away from this page, your interactions are governed strictly by the privacy policies of the destination platforms.

4. Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use functional performance cookies strictly to verify campaign routing and referral integrity. You can disable or erase cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding data processing under the ARCHES Framework, please reach out through our official communication channels at ARCHES Research Centre.
