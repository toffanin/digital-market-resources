Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 • ARCHES Innovation Ecosystem Framework

This Privacy Policy outlines how the ARCHES Innovation Ecosystem ("we," "our," or "us") handles data, cookies, and user privacy when you visit our promotional bridge pages and informational portals.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. Depending on how you interact with our bridge page, we may process:

  • Technical Log Data: Standard server logs including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps.
  • Analytics & Performance Data: Anonymized usage statistics to measure page performance and outbound click traffic.
  • Voluntary Communications: Any information you provide directly if you contact us via email or contact forms.

2. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Services

This website serves as an independent promotional bridge page for enterprise technology tools, including Instapage. When you click on outbound links (such as CTA buttons directing to https://get.instapage.com/...), you are redirected to third-party platforms.

Please note:

  • Third-party platforms utilize their own tracking cookies and privacy policies to attribute referral commissions and manage account registrations.
  • We do not store, collect, or have access to payment details, credit card numbers, or account credentials entered on third-party partner websites.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use standard essential and analytical cookies to evaluate page performance and ensure proper routing of referral links. You can control or disable cookie settings directly through your web browser preferences at any time.

4. Data Protection & GDPR Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and international privacy standards, you hold specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request details on any personal data stored about you.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of any personal communications or data held by us.
  • Right to Object: Object to standard log processing or analytical evaluation.

5. External Links Disclaimer

Our bridge pages contain outbound links to external sites not operated by ARCHES. We strongly advise reviewing the individual Privacy Policy and Terms of Service of every external site you visit.
