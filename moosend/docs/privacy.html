In accordance with EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR), users residing within the European Economic Area (EEA) possess specific rights regarding personal data processing:

  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request the removal of any direct email inquiries sent to our editorial staff.
  • Right to Object: Restrict or object to standard analytical logging procedures.
